# Expertise AI

> GTM experts productize their know-how as protected, runnable agents. Businesses discover those agents and run them through Expertise Assist.

Expertise AI keeps four paths distinct: the Expert Network is where GTM experts productize protected agents; the Agents directory is how businesses discover those agents; Expertise Assist runs selected agents on business data; and Expertise Live is the separate product for real-time website conversations that qualify and route buyers.

## Glossary

- [GTM glossary](https://www.expertise.ai/glossary): AI sales terms defined with verified, sourced statistics
- [Chatbot vs AI Sales Agent](https://www.expertise.ai/glossary/chatbot-vs-ai-sales-agent): A chatbot answers questions from a script. An AI sales agent runs qualified buying conversations, books meetings and triggers follow-up. See the numbers.
- [What Is an AI SDR?](https://www.expertise.ai/glossary/what-is-an-ai-sdr): An AI SDR is an autonomous agent that does sales development work: sourcing, outreach, qualification and booking. Definition plus verified 2026 data.
- [AI SDR vs Human SDR](https://www.expertise.ai/glossary/ai-sdr-vs-human-sdr): Verified numbers on AI vs human SDRs: cost per meeting, show rates, reply rates, ramp — and the 70/30 split winning teams converge on.
- [Company-Level vs Person-Level Visitor Identification](https://www.expertise.ai/glossary/company-level-vs-person-level-visitor-identification): Company-level identification tells you which account is on your site; person-level names the individual. How each works and the compliance difference.
- [What Is Speed to Lead?](https://www.expertise.ai/glossary/what-is-speed-to-lead): Speed to lead is the time from a lead's first hand-raise to your first meaningful response. Definition plus the verified decay curve: 21x, 8x and 42 hours.
- [What Is Signal Activation?](https://www.expertise.ai/glossary/what-is-signal-activation): Signal activation turns intent signals, like a visit, a pricing-page view or a hand-raise, into an immediate, contextual next action. See how it works.
- [What Is Warm Outbound?](https://www.expertise.ai/glossary/what-is-warm-outbound): Warm outbound is outreach triggered by a real buying signal, like an identified website visit, instead of a bought list. Definition and verified numbers.
- [Third-Party Intent Data vs Website Signals](https://www.expertise.ai/glossary/intent-data-vs-website-signals): Third-party intent data infers interest from activity elsewhere; first-party website signals observe it on your site. Why first-party wins on precision.
- [What Is a Qualified Conversation?](https://www.expertise.ai/glossary/what-is-a-qualified-conversation): A qualified conversation is a real-time exchange that establishes fit, need and next step: the missing unit between a website visit and a held meeting.
